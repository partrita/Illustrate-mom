Welcome to Molecule of the Month

  1. Load a structure — type a PDB ID (e.g. 2HHB) and press Fetch, or drop a file on the canvas.
  2. Move around — drag to rotate, Shift+drag to pan, scroll to zoom.
  3. Pick a style — click a preset card; press Ctrl+Z to undo.
  4. Export — Ctrl+E opens PNG size and DPI options. Press ? for all shortcuts.
